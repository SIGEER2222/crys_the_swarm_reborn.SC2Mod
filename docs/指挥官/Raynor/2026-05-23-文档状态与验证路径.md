```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\validate-raynor-port.ps1 -RequireXMFinalDependency -RequireLauncherCandidate
pwsh -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\mod-index.ps1 validate
```

实机验证建议只做一次集中验证：

```powershell
taskkill /f /im "SC2_x64.exe"
& "E:\SC2\SC2new\StarCraft II\Support64\SC2Switcher_x64.exe" "E:\SC2\SC2new\StarCraft II\Maps\XM\Launcher.SC2Map"
```

1. Launcher 可见 Raynor 候选并能写入 `CampaignXCore.SC2Bank`。
